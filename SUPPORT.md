# Support

MTG Image Archiver is maintained as an independent project. Support is provided when practical, but
no response time or continued-maintenance guarantee is offered.

## Report a bug

Use **About → Report a Bug** inside the application, or email
[ray.mtgarchiver@gmail.com](mailto:ray.mtgarchiver@gmail.com) with a subject beginning with
`[Bug]`.

Include:

- the application version and release channel shown on the About page;
- your Windows version and whether the application was installed or run in development;
- concise steps that reproduce the problem;
- the expected and actual results;
- relevant screenshots; and
- sanitized diagnostic information, when useful.

Review every message and attachment before sending it. Remove private paths, archive or card names,
email addresses, credentials, access tokens, and any other information you do not want to disclose.

## Suggest an improvement

Use **About → Suggest an Improvement**, or email
[ray.mtgarchiver@gmail.com](mailto:ray.mtgarchiver@gmail.com) with a subject beginning with
`[Suggestion]`.

Describe the problem you are trying to solve, your preferred behavior, and an example workflow.
Suggestions may be considered without creating an obligation to implement them.

## Installation or update problems

Before reporting an installation or update problem:

1. Confirm that the installer came from the
   [official Releases page](https://github.com/ray3331/mtg-image-archiver-releases/releases).
2. Verify its digest using [VERIFY_DOWNLOADS.md](VERIFY_DOWNLOADS.md).
3. Record the exact error message and the step where it occurred.
4. Mention whether another installed or development copy of the application was running.

Do not post suspected vulnerabilities publicly. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
